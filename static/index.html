THE ASSESSMENT, EXPLAINED

From profile to perspective.

01

Build the profile

Enter the applicant’s income, loan details and credit history. Use annual income and loan amounts in USD, matching the model’s dataset. The loan-to-income feature is calculated automatically and rounded to two decimals.

02

Run the assessment

Your saved, calibrated XGBoost model estimates the probability that the applicant defaults. No new model is trained during an assessment.

03

Read the result in context

A probability at or above the saved decision threshold is classified as high risk; a probability below it is classified as low risk. The displayed percentage is an estimate, not a guarantee or approval recommendation.

Changing any input clears the previous result. Download an assessment to keep a copy; this app does not store assessment history.
